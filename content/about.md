I'm Karol. Born in Chile, raised in New York — which means I get to be a local and a tourist at the same time.

Andean Road is my personal guide to Chile: the places I actually go, the things I'd tell you over dinner if you said you were finally taking the trip. Every place on this site is somewhere I've been myself. If I haven't been, it isn't here.

The site is small on purpose. No ads, no cookies, nothing following you around — just the guide.
