This is the whole guide as geography rather than as a list. Chile is 4,300
kilometres long and reads badly as an index — the useful question is almost
never "what is on this site" but "what is near the thing I already care about",
and that is a question only a map answers.

Every pin here has a page behind it, and every place page carries this same map,
centred on itself with its neighbours around it. They are one directory seen
from two distances.

Nothing here is aggregated, scraped or sponsored. It is one person's list of
places worth the trip, and it is short on purpose.
