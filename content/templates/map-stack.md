The map is an establishing shot: it answers *where are we* in one glance, then
gets out of the way. Everything below is ordinary page content at the normal
measure, so long-form writing sits comfortably under it.

Use this when the places are the point and the map is context. Use **Browse by
map** instead when the reader is scanning and comparing, and the map should stay
on screen the whole time.
