A planned route reads differently from a list of places: the **order matters**,
and the line between stops is information. The map draws the sequence with a
cased line so it stays visible over roads and terrain, and each stop is numbered
to match the itinerary below.

Numbers on the map and numbers in the list are the same numbers — that pairing is
what makes a route map legible.
