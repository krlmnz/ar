An area guide answers a question a place list can't: **which part of this country
do I want?** The map shows all the areas at once so their relationship is
obvious, then each band below belongs to one area and carries its colour.

The colour is the through-line — pin, legend swatch, band marker and card icon
all share it, so a reader can move between map and text without re-orienting.
