Run by a couple from Punta del Este, Uruguay, who brought their wood-fire grilling tradition to Pucón. The bife de chorizo is outstanding, the chimichurri is house-made, and the owners are usually there.

## What to expect

Small, warm restaurant with serious steak. The Uruguayan parrilla tradition is slightly different from Argentine — more emphasis on slow wood-fire rather than quick charcoal. You can taste the difference.

## Pro tips

Reserve ahead — it's always full, and it's the best meal in town. Go after a day of hiking or hot springs. You'll have earned it.
