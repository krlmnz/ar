This is where you go if you want to see what happens when Chilean winemakers stop following the rules. Father-son team making wines in clay pots — orange wines, wild blends, noble rot experiments. The father literally put Casablanca Valley on the map in the 1980s. RE stands for Rebirth, Reinvention, Rejuvenation.

## What to expect

The tasting is more educational than most — they'll explain the amphora process and why they're going back to ancient methods. The wines taste nothing like what you'd expect from Chile.

## Pro tips

If you've already been to a couple traditional wineries, save this one for contrast. It's a palate reset.
