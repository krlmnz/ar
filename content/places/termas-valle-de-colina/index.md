Terraced natural pools at high altitude with views that make you forget you're a few hours from a major city. The drive up is part of the adventure — winding mountain roads through increasingly dramatic Andean scenery.

## What to expect

Multiple pools at different temperatures carved into the mountainside. The higher pools are hotter. The views are genuinely spectacular — snow-capped peaks all around you. It's rustic, not a spa — think natural rock pools, not lounge chairs.

## Pro tips

Bring layers — the temperature drops fast up there. Go on a weekday if possible, weekends get crowded. The road requires a high-clearance vehicle for the last stretch.

## Getting there

From Santiago, head through Cajón del Maipo. The drive is 2.5 to 3 hours. The last portion is unpaved and steep — check conditions before going, especially in winter.
