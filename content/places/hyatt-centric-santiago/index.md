More of an executive contemporary vibe. The concierge was extremely helpful when I needed a car for the day — they just handled it. WiFi is reliable, I took a work call from the lobby with no issues.

## What to expect

Clean, modern rooms. Not as flashy as the W but everything works perfectly. The location in El Golf puts you walking distance to excellent restaurants and shopping on Isidora Goyenechea.

## Pro tips

If you need a car for a day trip, ask the concierge rather than booking online. They have relationships with reliable drivers and can often get you a better deal.
