For terracotta pottery and delicious authentic food, I recommend a day trip to Pomaire. It is a small town with a rich, natural clay deposit which has created a multigenerational pottery industry. It's a special working village of artisans who make pottery.

## What to expect

The shopping area is primarily one road. There are parking garages. Pomaire's crafts are on the less expensive side while still being quality and more rustic. I've brought back clay pots for making cazuela, beautiful mugs, small pots and more. Many have broken over the years, but I keep buying them because they are so inexpensive and well crafted.

## Pro tips

Buy a traditional chanchito for good luck. Even better, get 10 of them for $1,000 pesos / $2 to give as gifts. Beyond shopping, they are known for serving rustic and authentic Chilean food in oversized proportions.

## Skip if

You need everything to survive international shipping. The pottery is fragile. Pack well — it is very likely to break on the way home.
