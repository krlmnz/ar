The best rooftop in Santiago, with the most beautiful and exclusive views of the city. The atmosphere is trendy, young and social. You never know who you might see.

## What to expect

The rooftop pool overlooking the Andes is, in my opinion, the best view in all of Santiago. It is modern, high-end and on the street with some of the best shops and restaurants in the area. El Golf is known as "Sanhattan" — Santiago's answer to Manhattan — and the W is right in the center of it.

## Pro tips

Request a room with a mountain view. The difference between city-facing and Andes-facing rooms is night and day. The rooftop gets busy on weekends — go early afternoon for the best lounge chairs.

## Skip if

You want something quieter or more traditional. The W is social, buzzy, and unapologetically modern. If that's not your vibe, look at the Mandarin Oriental instead.
