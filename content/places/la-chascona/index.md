Pablo Neruda's Santiago house, built for his lover Matilde — named after her wild curly hair. He designed it like a ship, with a dining room styled as a captain's cabin. Neruda collected everything: ship figureheads, insects under glass, bottles, maps. It's eccentric, intimate, and worth seeing even if you don't know his poetry.

## What to expect

The house is in Bellavista, a colorful neighborhood at the base of Cerro San Cristóbal. The audio guide walks you through the rooms and Neruda's life. It takes about an hour.

## Pro tips

Combine it with a walk through Bellavista and lunch in the neighborhood. If you're doing the full Neruda trail (three houses, three cities), this is the most accessible starting point.
