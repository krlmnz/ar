A fully biodynamic winery between Casablanca and the coast, about 13km from the Pacific. The wines are exceptional — Syrah, Pinot Noir, cool-climate whites — and they have a restaurant that cooks from their own organic garden. If you want to stay, the colonial-era Casona is a beautiful overnight.

## What to expect

This is the most complete winery experience in the valley. Tour the vineyards, taste the wines, eat at the restaurant, and if you have time, stay the night. The Pacific influence on the wines is real — you can taste the coastal fog.

## Pro tips

If you're doing a wine day trip, make Matetic your last stop. It's the farthest from Santiago and closest to the coast — you can drive to the beach after.
