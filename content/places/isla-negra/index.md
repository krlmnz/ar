Not actually an island — a quiet coastal stretch south of Valparaíso where Neruda built his favorite home, shaped like a boat looking out to sea. He and Matilde are buried here on a hilltop above the Pacific. The house is filled with his obsessive collections: shells, ship figureheads, bottles, maps. It's the most personal of his three museum homes.

## What to expect

The house feels different from La Chascona — quieter, more reflective. The ocean is right there. You can hear the waves as you walk through the rooms. The collections are extraordinary — Neruda was a compulsive collector of everything maritime.

## Pro tips

Combine with a wine day in Casablanca Valley. Isla Negra is about 30 minutes past the valley toward the coast. Go in the afternoon after your winery visits.
