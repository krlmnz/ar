Tucked deep inside Villarrica National Park, Termas Geométricas feels like stepping into a dream. A wooden red walkway winding through lush forest and rising steam connects 17+ warm pools from 95°F to 113°F. The pools are set in a canyon with a natural, freezing cold waterfall for a stark contrast cold-plunge.

## What to expect

Nearby Volcán Villarrica creates the thermal baths — one of the most relaxing experiences and a highlight of Chile's abundant natural beauty. A small cafeteria inside serves lunch foods and snacks. Plan to spend 3-4 hours minimum.

## Pro tips

Book a trip with transportation back and forth from Pucón. Bring a swimsuit, a towel, cash and a water bottle. You must have an ID to enter. No alcoholic beverages — but no one will check what's in your water bottle ;)

## Skip if

You want a quick dip. The drive is too long to rush. This deserves a full afternoon.
