A boutique family winery on land they've owned for over a century. Small-production Pinot Noir and Syrah, named Winery of the Year by Wine & Spirits Magazine. The modern tasting salon is beautiful and the tours feel personal, not corporate.

## What to expect

This is not a big commercial operation. You'll likely meet someone from the family or their small team. The wines are exceptional — especially the Pinot Noir and Syrah — and the tasting experience feels like visiting someone's estate, because it is.

## Pro tips

Pair this with Casa del Bosque for a perfect two-winery day. Kingston is more intimate, Casa del Bosque has the better restaurant.
