Definitely try to get a reservation. The chef is a local celebrity with an amazing blend of Chilean, Thai, Peruvian, and French cuisine. It's delish.

## What to expect

Intimate setting in the heart of Lastarria. The menu changes with the seasons and whatever's fresh at La Vega market. Expect bold flavors and unexpected combinations that somehow work perfectly.

## Pro tips

Book at least a few days ahead, especially for weekend dinners. Sit outside if the weather allows — Lastarria's street scene is part of the experience.
