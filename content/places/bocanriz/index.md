One of the best wine bars in Santiago, with an enormous by-the-glass selection that could double as a crash course in Chilean wine. If you want to explore different regions and grapes without committing to full bottles, start here.

## What to expect

The menu is organized by region, which makes it easy to compare a Casablanca Valley Sauvignon Blanc against a Leyda one, or taste your way across Maipo and Colchagua reds. Staff are knowledgeable and happy to guide you.

## Pro tips

Go on a weeknight. Weekends get packed. Ask for a flight organized by region — it's the best way to understand Chilean wine geography in one sitting. The food menu is solid too, designed to pair.

## Skip if

You're not into wine. This is literally a wine bar. Go to Chipe Libre instead for pisco cocktails.
