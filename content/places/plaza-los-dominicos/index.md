An outdoor market with the best selection of handmade, high quality leather and woven goods. Inside, my favorite is Artesania de Chile where you can find rugs and tapestries of all sizes. It is not the cheapest but it is West Elm aesthetics and quality for way less pesos. Definitely worth bringing back, even if you have to pay for extra luggage.

## What to expect

Walking around you'll find fine leather goods like belts, handbags and traditional huaso hats. Copper goods are also very cheap in Chile if you're looking to upgrade your cookware.

## Pro tips

For scarves and woven clothing, look for the store with a large table loom where you'll see their products being made. I always get a few baby alpaca scarves there. So soft and in very modern, minimalist colors.

## Getting there

It's in the Las Condes area but a bit further east from the main hotel zone. A quick Uber ride from El Golf. You'll want about 2-3 hours to browse properly.
