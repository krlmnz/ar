Santiago's big contemporary arts center — theater, dance, music, visual art — all under one roof. It's free to walk around and there's always something on. If you're staying in Lastarria, you'll walk past it constantly.

## What to expect

The building itself is architecturally striking. Inside, rotating exhibitions cover everything from photography to installation art. Check their schedule — live performances happen most evenings.

## Pro tips

The ground floor cafe is a good spot to work or people-watch. The building is open late and the surrounding plaza has food trucks on weekends.
