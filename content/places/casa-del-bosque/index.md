Their restaurant Tanino is excellent — a privileged atmosphere surrounded by vineyard views. The tour of the winemaking process is genuinely interesting, and they have a tasting house up a hill with panoramic views of the entire valley.

## What to expect

The tour takes about 90 minutes and covers the vineyard, the barrel room, and ends at the tasting house on the hill. The guide speaks English and Spanish. The tasting includes their signature wines — the Sauvignon Blanc is exceptional.

## Pro tips

Book the 11am tour so you can have lunch at Tanino right after. Request the terrace table if the weather's good — the vineyard view from there is the money shot. If you're visiting multiple wineries, do Casa del Bosque first. It has the best restaurant, and you want to be hungry for it.

## Getting there

From Santiago, take Ruta 68 toward Valparaíso. The winery is signposted off the highway about 20 minutes before the Casablanca town exit. The road is paved all the way.

## Skip if

You're not into wine, or you've already hit 3 wineries today and your palate is shot. At that point, just go to the coast.
