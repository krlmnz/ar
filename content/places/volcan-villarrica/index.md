Known in the Mapuche tongue as Rucapillán — house of the great spirit — this active volcano is a centerpiece of Villarrica National Park and a major adventure attraction. Climbing it is highly worth it for panoramic views of the Andes and surrounding lakes.

## What to expect

It takes 4-6 hours to climb and it is physically challenging, requires special gear, a healthy abled body, stamina and a determined, patient mindset. It also has the unique thrill of standing on the rim of an active, fuming crater if you are into that sort of thing. You can find guided hikes to the 2,847-meter summit year-round.

## Pro tips

Book a guided tour in Pucón's town center in advance — they need to fit you for gear including crampons, an ice axe, and a helmet. Start early. The weather window is best in the morning before afternoon clouds roll in.

## Skip if

You're not in good physical shape or you're uncomfortable with heights. This is a real climb, not a walk. The altitude and steepness are no joke.
