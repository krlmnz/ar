A rainforest park about 40 minutes from Pucón with trails along lakes, waterfalls, and ancient araucaria — monkey puzzle trees that look like they belong in a Dr. Seuss book. The higher you climb, the more otherworldly it gets. One of the best day hikes in the region.

## What to expect

Multiple trail options from easy lakeside walks to more challenging climbs to the upper lakes. The araucaria trees are thousands of years old and look like nothing you've seen before. On clear days, you can see Volcán Villarrica from the higher viewpoints.

## Pro tips

Start early to beat the afternoon crowds. The Los Lagos trail is the classic route — about 4-5 hours round trip to the upper lakes. Bring plenty of water and snacks.
