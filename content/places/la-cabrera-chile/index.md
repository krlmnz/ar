I can't express how good the steak is. An Argentine steakhouse that does not mess around. The cuts are massive, perfectly grilled, and the sides keep coming.

## What to expect

If you love steak, this is your spot in Santiago. It's one of the highest-rated restaurants in Las Condes for a reason. The portions are generous and the quality is outstanding.

## Pro tips

Go hungry. The sides alone could be a meal. If you're not sure how to order your steak in Spanish, ask for "al punto" for medium-rare.
