Picturesque wine country sitting between the Andes and the Pacific. An easy day trip from Santiago — you can hit two or three wineries and still make it to the coast for sunset.

## Route suggestion

Start with **Casa del Bosque** for the 11am tour and lunch at Tanino. Then head to **Kingston Family** or **Bodegas RE** for an afternoon tasting. If you want to linger near the coast, finish at **Matetic** — it's the closest to the Pacific and has a beautiful restaurant.

## What you need to know

Book tastings at least a day ahead, especially on weekends. Most wineries close by 5pm. You'll want a designated driver or hire one — a private driver from Santiago runs about $80-100 for the day and is totally worth it.

## Skip if

You don't drink wine. There's not much else to do in the valley. Head to the coast instead.
