Pucón is Chile's adventure capital — a small town at the base of an active volcano, on the shore of a lake, surrounded by ancient rainforest and volcanic hot springs. It's where you go when you want your trip to be a story.

## The itinerary

**Day 1:** Arrive and settle in. Walk the town, book your volcano climb for Day 2, and have dinner at **La Maga** — you'll need the calories.

**Day 2:** Climb **Volcán Villarrica**. 4-6 hours up, 2 hours down. You'll stand on the rim of an active, fuming crater. Celebrate with a long nap.

**Day 3:** **Termas Geométricas** — the most beautiful hot springs you'll ever see. 17 volcanic pools connected by a red walkway through rainforest and steam. You've earned this.

**Day 4:** Hike **Parque Nacional Huerquehue**. Ancient monkey puzzle trees, emerald lakes, and trails that get more magical the higher you go.

## What you need to know

Book the volcano climb at least a day ahead — they need to fit you for gear. Termas Geométricas requires advance booking too. A rental car makes everything easier, but tours from Pucón are readily available.
