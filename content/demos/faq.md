Short answers. If a question needs an essay, it wants a different template.

## Do I need a car?

In Santiago, no. The day you leave the basin, yes. A car you pick up in the city and drop at the airport is the version with the least logistics.

## Can I drink the water?

Yes, in the cities. On the road, people still buy bottles, mostly out of habit and because the gas stations are cold.

## How late is dinner?

Ten is normal. Nine is early. A reservation at seven will be you, the staff, and the bread.

## Cash or card?

Card almost everywhere you are a visitor. Cash in the pottery town, the smaller vineyards, and anywhere the sign is handwritten.

## What do I skip if I only have a weekend?

The south. Give the weekend to the city and one road out of it — the valley or the coast, not both.
