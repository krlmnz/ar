The first time you eat at the hour the room expects, the day rearranges itself. Lunch was a placeholder. This is the meal the kitchen cooked for.

You do not need a speech about it. You need a table you can stay at, and the sense to let the bread arrive before you have decided anything.

## What to order when you can't decide

Point at whatever is written in the smallest letters. It is usually the thing they hope a regular will ask for. If there is a stew, it has been ready longer than you have been hungry, which is a compliment.

Leave before you are offered a third drink or after, but not during. The walk home is part of the meal, and the cordillera is still a dark edge above the streetlights if you look up at the right corner.
