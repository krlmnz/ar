The trip is short. The mistake is treating it like an errand you can start at noon.

## You'll need

- Cash, even if the vineyard says it takes cards
- A layer you can take off in the car and put on at the coast
- Water you packed, not water you plan to buy at the first stop

## Do this

1. Leave while the avenues are still a suggestion.
2. Make one stop. The second stop is how a day trip becomes a drive.
3. Turn around when the light is still on the hills, not after you have negotiated with it.

> The last bakery that is actually good is earlier on the road than the one with the sign.
