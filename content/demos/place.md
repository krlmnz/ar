Pomaire still throws pots the way it did when the road was the reason to come, not the obstacle. The main street is short. The smell is clay, then frying, then clay again.

Go for the town, not a single shop. The good pieces are in the doorways, and the person who made them is usually the person who wraps them.

## What to expect

A half day is the right size. Walk until the street ends, eat, walk back on the other side. If you are with children, the kilns are more interesting than any museum explanation you could give them.

## Skip if

You want a quiet gallery. This is a working street, and Saturday sounds like one.
